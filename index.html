What is TerraTemp?

A real-time Earth temperature monitoring platform that aggregates global sensor data into a single, unified view.

How it works

Surface and atmospheric readings are collected, normalized, and mapped onto the globe — updated continuously.

Why it matters

Tracking planetary temperature shifts helps researchers and policymakers understand climate change at a global scale.
